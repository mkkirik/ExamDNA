## Section B

**6.** [6 marks]
- Final answer: **(a) 1840 W (b) 2.8 × 10^5 J (c) yes: total current 14 A exceeds 13 A**

  *Worked solution:* (a) P = VI = 230 × 8.0 = 1840 W. (b) E = Pt = 1840 × 150 = 276 000 J ≈ 2.8 × 10⁵ J. (c) I_total = 8.0 + 6.0 = 14 A > 13 A, so the fuse melts.

**7.** [7 marks]
- Final answer: **(a) 1.0 × 10^4 N (10 170 N) (b) 2.4 m/s² (c) safe: 1.5 < 2.4 m/s²**

  *Worked solution:* (a) T = m(g + a) = 900 × 11.3 = 10 170 N. (b) a_max = Tmax/m − g = 11 000/900 − 9.8 = 2.42 m/s². (c) 1.5 m/s² < 2.4 m/s², so safe.

**8.** [7 marks]

  *Worked solution:* (a) mgh = 20 × 9.8 × 5.0 = 980 J. (b) gradient = 2000/10 = 200 W. (c) efficiency = 980/2000 = 0.49.
