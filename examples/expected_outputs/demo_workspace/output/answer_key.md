## Section B

**6.** [6 marks]
- Final answer: **(a) 1840 W (b) 2.8 × 10^5 J (c) yes: total current 14 A exceeds 13 A**

  *Worked solution:* (a) P = VI = 230 × 8.0 = 1840 W. (b) E = Pt = 1840 × 150 = 276 000 J ≈ 2.8 × 10⁵ J. (c) I_total = 8.0 + 6.0 = 14 A > 13 A, so the fuse melts.

**7.** [7 marks]
- Final answer: **(a) 3.2 m/s² (b) 6.2 s (c) less friction on ice, so smaller deceleration and longer distance**

  *Worked solution:* (a) a = u²/(2s) = 400/124 = 3.2 m/s² (b) t = u/a = 20/3.23 = 6.2 s (c) Ice reduces friction, so the maximum deceleration is smaller and the car travels further.

**8.** [7 marks]

  *Worked solution:* (a) mgh = 20 × 9.8 × 5.0 = 980 J. (b) gradient = 2000/10 = 200 W. (c) efficiency = 980/2000 = 0.49.
