# Mini Syllabus — Introductory Physics (synthetic)

Original synthetic syllabus written for the ExamDNA demonstration. Not based on any examination board's specification.

## Units and quantities
- **U1** Distinguish scalar and vector quantities and use SI units.

## Kinematics
- **K1** Use the equations of uniformly accelerated motion.
- **K2** Interpret motion data in tables and graphs (gradient as acceleration, area as displacement).
- **K3** Analyse projectile motion with independent horizontal and vertical components.

## Forces
- **F1** Apply Newton's second law F = ma.
- **F2** Apply Hooke's law F = kx within the limit of proportionality.
- **F3** Explain motion using Newton's first law.

## Energy
- **E1** Calculate gravitational potential energy (mgh) and kinetic energy (½mv²); apply conservation of energy.
- **E2** Calculate power (P = E/t) and efficiency (useful ÷ total).

## Momentum
- **M1** Calculate momentum (p = mv) and apply conservation of momentum in one dimension.

## Waves
- **W1** Use the wave equation v = fλ.
- **W2** Describe refraction and calculate wave speed in a medium (v = c/n).

## Electricity
- **C1** Apply Ohm's law (V = IR) to single resistors and series circuits.
- **C2** Relate charge, current, potential difference and power (I = Q/t, P = VI).

## Assessment format (synthetic papers)
- Section A: short items (1–3 marks): multiple choice, short answer, single-step calculations.
- Section B: structured questions (6–8 marks) with parts, combining calculation and explanation.
- Take g = 9.8 m/s² unless stated.
