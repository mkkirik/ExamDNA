# Synthetic Physics Paper 3

Subject: Introductory Physics

Total: 32 marks. Answer all questions. (Original synthetic material created for ExamDNA.)

## Section A

1. Which of the following is the SI unit of power? [1 mark]
   A. joule
   B. newton
   C. watt
   D. pascal

2. A climber of mass 60 kg climbs a vertical height of 12 m. Calculate the gain in gravitational potential energy of the climber. Take g = 9.8 m/s². [2 marks]

3. A ball rolls off the edge of a horizontal table 1.8 m above the floor. Air resistance is negligible. Calculate the time taken for the ball to reach the floor. Take g = 9.8 m/s². [3 marks]

4. A wave on a stretched string travels at 12 m/s and has a wavelength of 0.40 m. Calculate the frequency of the wave. [2 marks]

5. The potential difference across a 220 Ω resistor is 11 V. Calculate the current in the resistor. [2 marks]

6. A block slides down the slope shown in the diagram. Calculate its acceleration. [2 marks]

## Section B

1. A lift of total mass 900 kg accelerates upwards at 1.5 m/s². Take g = 9.8 m/s².
(a) Calculate the weight of the lift. [1 marks]
(b) Calculate the tension in the lift cable. [3 marks]
(c) Explain why the tension is smaller when the lift moves upwards at constant speed. [3 marks]

2. An electric motor lifts a load of mass 25 kg through a vertical height of 4.0 m in 8.0 s. The input power to the motor is 200 W. Take g = 9.8 m/s².
(a) Calculate the useful energy transferred to the load. [2 marks]
(b) Calculate the total energy supplied to the motor. [2 marks]
(c) Calculate the efficiency of the motor. [3 marks]

3. Light travels from air into a glass block of refractive index 1.5. The speed of light in air is 3.0 × 10^8 m/s.
(a) Calculate the speed of light in the glass. [2 marks]
(b) Explain why a ray entering the glass at an angle changes direction. [2 marks]
(c) State what happens to the frequency of the light as it enters the glass, and give a reason. [2 marks]
