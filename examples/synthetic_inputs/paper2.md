# Synthetic Physics Paper 2

Subject: Introductory Physics

Total: 30 marks. Answer all questions. (Original synthetic material created for ExamDNA.)

## Section A

1. Which of the following is a correct unit for momentum? [1 mark]
   A. kg m/s
   B. kg m/s²
   C. N/s
   D. J s

2. A car of mass 1200 kg is moving at 15 m/s. Calculate the momentum of the car. [2 marks]

3. A spring with spring constant 40 N/m is extended by 0.15 m. Calculate the force applied to the spring. [2 marks]

4. A sound wave travels at 340 m/s and has a frequency of 170 Hz. What is its wavelength? [1 mark]
   A. 0.50 m
   B. 2.0 m
   C. 510 m
   D. 57 800 m

5. A charge of 30 C flows through a lamp in 60 s. Calculate the current in the lamp. [2 marks]

6. A puck slides across a frictionless horizontal surface. Explain why it continues to move at constant velocity. [2 marks]

## Section B

1. A trolley of mass 2.0 kg moving at 3.0 m/s collides with a stationary trolley of mass 1.0 kg. The trolleys stick together.
(a) Calculate the total momentum before the collision. [2 marks]
(b) Calculate the velocity of the trolleys immediately after the collision. [3 marks]
(c) Calculate the kinetic energy lost in the collision. [3 marks]

2. The table shows the velocity of a sprinter at different times after the start of a race.
(a) Calculate the acceleration of the sprinter during the first 4.0 s. [2 marks]
(b) Calculate the distance travelled in the first 6.0 s. [3 marks]
(c) Describe the motion of the sprinter between 4.0 s and 6.0 s. [1 marks]
   t / s | v / m s⁻¹
   0 | 0
   2.0 | 3.0
   4.0 | 6.0
   6.0 | 6.0

3. An electric kettle is connected to a 230 V mains supply and draws a current of 8.0 A.
(a) Calculate the power of the kettle. [2 marks]
(b) Calculate the energy transferred by the kettle in 120 s. [2 marks]
(c) Suggest why the kettle cable is thicker than the cable for a lamp. [2 marks]
