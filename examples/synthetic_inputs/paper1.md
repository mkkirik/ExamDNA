# Synthetic Physics Paper 1

Subject: Introductory Physics

Total: 30 marks. Answer all questions. (Original synthetic material created for ExamDNA.)

## Section A

1. Which of the following quantities is a vector? [1 mark]
   A. Speed
   B. Distance
   C. Displacement
   D. Time

2. A cyclist accelerates uniformly from rest to a speed of 8.0 m/s in 4.0 s. Calculate the acceleration of the cyclist. [2 marks]

3. A resultant force of 6.0 N acts on a trolley of mass 2.0 kg. Calculate the acceleration of the trolley. [2 marks]

4. A lamp transfers 120 J of energy in 4.0 s. Calculate the power of the lamp. [2 marks]

5. A wave has a frequency of 50 Hz and a wavelength of 2.0 m. What is the speed of the wave? [1 mark]
   A. 25 m/s
   B. 52 m/s
   C. 100 m/s
   D. 0.04 m/s

6. A current of 0.50 A flows through a 12 Ω resistor. Calculate the potential difference across the resistor. [2 marks]

## Section B

1. A car travelling at 20 m/s brakes with uniform deceleration and comes to rest in a distance of 50 m.
(a) Calculate the magnitude of the deceleration of the car. [3 marks]
(b) Calculate the time taken for the car to stop. [2 marks]
(c) Explain why the braking distance would be larger on a wet road. [2 marks]

2. A ball of mass 0.50 kg is dropped from rest from a height of 3.2 m. Air resistance is negligible. Take g = 9.8 m/s².
(a) Calculate the gravitational potential energy lost by the ball as it falls to the ground. [2 marks]
(b) Calculate the speed of the ball just before it hits the ground. [3 marks]
(c) Explain how the speed just before impact would change if air resistance were not negligible. [2 marks]

3. A 4.0 Ω resistor and a 6.0 Ω resistor are connected in series with a 12 V battery of negligible internal resistance.
(a) Calculate the total resistance of the circuit. [1 marks]
(b) Calculate the current in the circuit. [2 marks]
(c) Calculate the potential difference across the 6.0 Ω resistor. [3 marks]
